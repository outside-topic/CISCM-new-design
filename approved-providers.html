Approved Providers & Centres

Clear delivery authority.
Controlled responsibilities.

Provider and centre approvals define who may deliver which activities, where, how and under whose accountability.

✓CISCM

Two distinct approval categories.

01

Approved Training Provider

An accountable organization authorized to deliver named CISCM or CISCM-recognized programs within a defined scope.

02

Approved Delivery Centre

A named site operating under an approved parent or provider, with clear administrative and delivery responsibilities.

The approved scope should state.

  • Legal entity and approved trading name
  • Physical and virtual sites, territories and delivery modes
  • Subject domains, named programs and languages
  • Approved trainers, assessors or moderators where applicable
  • Technology, recruitment, academic, delivery and market partners

Control material changes.

Notify CISCM of changes that may affect authority or quality, including ownership, leadership, sites, platforms, countries, partners, programs, assessment, learner volume, closure, serious complaints, data incidents or regulatory action.

Approval should make responsibility for admissions, delivery, assessment, certification, complaints, records, data, marketing and corrective action explicit.
