Accessibility information for this website concept, with a route to request assistance or report a difficulty.

ACISCM

Access features in this design.

01

Keyboard access

Navigation, accordions, tabs, filters and forms use native controls, with visible focus states.

02

Responsive layout

Pages adjust across screen widths and allow tabular content to scroll when needed.

03

Readable structure

Headings, labelled form fields, skip links and descriptive navigation support orientation.

04

Reduced motion

The design respects the browser’s reduced-motion preference for scrolling and transitions.

Report an access issue.

Describe the page, device or browser, the difficulty encountered and the assistance you need. Avoid including unnecessary sensitive information.
