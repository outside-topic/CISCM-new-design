Appeals

Request an independent
review of a decision.

Use an appeal to challenge a formal CISCM decision. Confirm the applicable appeal policy and deadlines before acting.

ACISCM

Published recommended timeframes.

These timeframes are described as recommendations on the source page. Confirm the deadline and procedure applicable to your decision.
Stage Recommended timeframe Responsibility
Appeal submission Within 30 days of decision notification Appellant
Acknowledgement Within 5 working days CISCM
Reviewer appointment Within 15 working days CISCM
Appeal documentation Within 10 working days of appointment Appellant
Original decision response Within 15 working days Decision-maker
Review and determination Within 30 working days Appeal reviewer
Outcome communication Within 5 working days of determination CISCM

Confidentiality and decision independence.

Assessment, complaint and appeal information should be handled confidentially, with disclosure governed by consent and legal requirements. Personal-data requests are subject to applicable law.

Payment does not guarantee an approval, grade, credential or passing result.

Ask about the applicable appeal route.

Include the decision reference and date when requesting procedural guidance.
