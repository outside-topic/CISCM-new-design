CFS / Functional expertise

Build recognition around your specialist strength.

For practitioners who solve complex problems within a defined function and can explain the reasoning behind their decisions.

Competency focus
Level 3 · Analytical capability

Discuss your fit →

What you should demonstrate

  • Diagnose functional problems using relevant evidence.
  • Select suitable methods and interpret incomplete information.
  • Develop recommendations supported by clear reasoning.

Available specializations

Demand & Supply PlanningSupply Chain AnalyticsProduction and Operations PlanningIntegrated Business PlanningSupply Chain Network DesignSupply Chain Cost ManagementQuality and Supplier Performance

Assessment principle

Applied scenarios and analytical reasoning should establish competence in the selected units. The applicable credential specification determines the exact requirements.

CP / Professional practice

Connect functions. Deliver operational outcomes.

For professionals who manage processes, projects, teams or suppliers and understand how decisions affect the wider supply chain.

Competency focus
Level 3 in core domains
Level 2 in other domains

Discuss your fit →

What you should demonstrate

  • Apply supply chain methods to practical operational challenges.
  • Analyze service, cost, inventory, supplier and risk implications.
  • Make ethical, evidence-informed recommendations across functions.

Available specializations

Procurement & SourcingLogistics & TransportationInventory & WarehousingGlobal Trade & ComplianceContract and Commercial ManagementDistribution and Channel ManagementFreight and Carrier ManagementSustainable and Green Supply ChainSupply Chain Project Management

Assessment principle

Evidence should demonstrate applied competence and analysis across the relevant domains, following the published assessment and eligibility rules.

ChSCP / Strategic leadership

Turn enterprise complexity into defensible decisions.

For experienced leaders integrating strategy, operations, finance and governance across organizational boundaries.

Competency focus
Level 4 · Architectural capability
Across the majority of domains

Discuss your fit →

What you should demonstrate

  • Design networks, operating models, policies or governance systems.
  • Balance service, cost, cash, risk and sustainability trade-offs.
  • Defend enterprise decisions under uncertainty.
  • Translate analysis into adoption and sustained change.

Suitable professional profiles

Supply chain directorsSenior functional leadersTransformation leadersNetwork and operating-model architectsCross-functional program leaders

Evidence route

Advanced case analysis, portfolio evidence, professional discussion and verified experience may form part of the route, as defined by the approved handbook. Selected-response testing alone does not establish architectural competence.

This is a private professional designation. Title and post-nominal use follow CISCM policy; it is not a governmental or statutory charter.

Which credential is right for me?

Start with the scope of your role and the evidence you can provide. CFS emphasizes functional depth; CP emphasizes connected operational practice; ChSCP emphasizes system design and enterprise judgement. Check the applicable eligibility criteria before applying.

Do I have to complete a particular training course?

The credential framework does not prescribe one compulsory preparation route. Employment, self-study and structured learning may all contribute. Assessment and eligibility requirements still apply.

Does certification automatically include membership?

No automatic equivalence should be assumed. Certification and membership recognize different things, and the applicable policies determine whether either includes additional status or benefits.

How will my competence be assessed?

Methods should match the proficiency claimed. They may include scenarios, applied cases, practical evidence, portfolios or professional discussion, depending on the approved credential specification.

Where can I confirm fees, eligibility and renewal rules?

Request the current credential specification and certification handbook from CISCM. Confirm fees, assessment arrangements, reassessment and renewal terms before making an application.
