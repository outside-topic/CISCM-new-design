What activities may count?

Relevant courses, webinars, projects, research, mentoring, teaching, writing, conferences, professional service and guided self-study may count when supported by evidence and linked to a development need.

What should my CPD record contain?

Record the date, activity and source; relevant competency domain or unit; hours and activity type; learning gained; application or reflection; and supporting evidence.

How do I choose a membership grade?

Compare your current professional responsibilities, experience and contribution with the grade profiles. Ask CISCM to confirm the applicable criteria and evidence before applying.

Is Chartered Member the same as ChSCP certification?

No. Membership grades and certification credentials have separate purposes and rules. The permitted title or designation is determined by the specific approved award or membership policy.

Are the CPD hours shown a final requirement?

The source page describes these as recommended expectations. The approved policy for your grade determines the final CPD requirements.

Does organizational membership approve our training programs?

No. Organizational membership does not itself approve training providers, programs, trainers or certificate issuance. Separate documented authorization is required.

Where can I confirm membership fees and renewal terms?

Request current information from CISCM before applying. No fee or renewal period has been assumed in this design.

Can I progress to another membership grade?

Progression is based on demonstrated competence, ethical standing and contribution under the applicable grade policy. Length of membership alone does not establish eligibility.
