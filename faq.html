13 questions

What is CISCM?

About CISCM
A Texas-registered independent professional organization specializing in supply chain, logistics, procurement and operations management.

What does the Body of Knowledge contain?

Standards
Four currents, fourteen domains, seventy-two competency units, five cumulative proficiency levels and six cross-cutting threads. The Second Edition includes commentary, knowledge elements, performance criteria, worked examples and characteristic failure modes.

Is a particular course mandatory?

Credentials
No single preparation route is prescribed. Employment, self-study, employer learning, academic study and recognized providers may support preparation, subject to credential requirements.

How is competence assessed?

Credentials
Assessment methods are selected for the outcome and proficiency level. They may include scenarios, applied cases, observation, simulations, portfolios, projects and professional discussion.

Is membership the same as certification?

Membership
Certification evidences assessed competence. Membership reflects continuing professional standing under its own rules. Neither should be assumed to automatically grant the other.

Does recognition guarantee a job or promotion?

Credentials
No. Credentials do not guarantee employment, promotion, salary, immigration approval, academic admission or regulatory acceptance.

What does accreditation cover?

Accreditation
The exact entity, program, site, mode, language, territory and validity specified in the formal approval record.

Does CISCM accreditation grant government or degree authority?

Accreditation
No such status should be implied without express confirmation by an independently competent authority.

Which membership grades are described?

Membership
Associate Member, Professional Member, Chartered Member and Fellow. Eligibility, approved designations and grade rules must be confirmed against the current policy.

What are the CPD expectations?

CPD
The published recommendation for professional grades is 30 relevant hours annually, including 15 structured hours and two hours addressing professional responsibility. The approved grade policy determines the final requirements.

What activities may count?

CPD
Relevant courses, webinars, projects, research, mentoring, teaching, writing, conferences, professional service and guided self-study, supported by evidence and an identified development need.

How can I check a recognition claim?

Integrity
Check the holder, recognition title, scope, validity and status with CISCM. The preview’s verification page prepares an enquiry and does not access a live register.

What responsibilities do members have?

Integrity
Act honestly and competently, safeguard information, manage conflicts, maintain competence, use recognition correctly, disclose relevant matters and cooperate with verification or complaints processes.
