An institution-wide perspective

Recognize the system
behind the learning.

Institutional accreditation examines an organization’s ability to govern, deliver, assess and improve professional learning within an agreed scope.

It considers how your quality arrangements work in practice across the locations, delivery modes and activities included in the review.

Evidence at every level.

01Legal identity and integrity

Verify legal standing, ownership, relevant permissions, accurate claims and continuity arrangements.

Illustrative evidenceRegistration records, an ownership statement and a register of applicable licences.

02Governance and leadership

Establish responsibilities, delegated authority, oversight, risk management and leadership review.

Illustrative evidenceOrganization chart, committee terms and management review records.

03Quality management

Control policies, monitor performance, resolve complaints and track corrective improvement.

Illustrative evidencePolicy control log, internal review results and completed corrective actions.

04Program governance

Manage program approval, updates, periodic review and continuity for learners.

Illustrative evidenceApproval minutes, program review files and change-control records.

05Learner lifecycle

Manage entry, induction, accessibility, learner support, progress and completion records.

Illustrative evidenceAdmission records, induction materials and evidence of support interventions.

06Trainers and staff

Verify competence and manage induction, observation, professional development and workload.

Illustrative evidenceVerified staff profiles, observation records and development plans.

07Learning resources

Maintain suitable facilities, platforms, materials, accessibility and technology continuity.

Illustrative evidencePlatform demonstrations, resource reviews and service continuity arrangements.

08Assessment integrity

Control assessment design, authorship, security, marking, moderation and appeals.

Illustrative evidenceAssessment blueprints, anonymized marked work and moderation records.

09Certification and records

Control award authorization, learner identity, identifiers, retention and verification.

Illustrative evidenceIssue logs, sample verification records and retention controls.

10Partnership control

Oversee partners, contracts, subcontracting, branding, data and learner protection.

Illustrative evidenceDue-diligence files, contracts and partner monitoring records.

11Data and technology

Manage privacy, cybersecurity, system access, backup, incidents and responsible AI use.

Illustrative evidenceAccess reviews, backup evidence and incident-management records.

12Outcomes and improvement

Use completion, achievement, feedback, equity and impact data to improve provision.

Illustrative evidencePerformance trends, feedback analysis and tracked improvement actions.

Evidence examples are preparation suggestions for this design. Confirm the required evidence against CISCM’s current review criteria and requested scope.

Decision and validity

3 years

Recommended standard approval period

The published framework recommends a three-year period with annual surveillance.

A shorter period or additional monitoring may apply where risk, conditions or recent changes justify it.

The approval letter and register determine final validity. Confirm the duration, conditions and scope for your institution.

Ask CISCM a question →
How is institutional accreditation different from program accreditation?

Institutional review focuses on governance and quality systems within a defined scope. Program accreditation reviews a specific learning program. A program may be reviewed independently or alongside institutional accreditation.

Can our corporate academy apply?

Corporate academies are among the institution types described on the CISCM page. The category and scope should reflect the workforce, business units and programs under review.

Are written policies enough?

No. Prepare evidence that shows the policies operate consistently in practice across the requested scope.

Will the review include a site visit?

Validation depends on risk and scope. It may include facilities inspection, system demonstration, interviews, delivery observation and record sampling. Confirm the arrangements with CISCM.

Is the approval automatically valid for three years?

The framework recommends three years with annual surveillance. The actual validity, conditions and monitoring are established in the approval letter and register.

Does recognition cover every location and program?

Recognition is scope-specific. The sites, modes, territories, domains or programs covered depend on the category and formal approval documentation. Confirm what is authorized before making public claims.
