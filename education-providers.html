Independent decisions matter.

Developmental review or consultancy is not accreditation. Where advice is followed by a recognition decision, independent review and conflict safeguards should be used. The provider remains responsible for its own system.

Recognized program coverage does not make attendance on one provider’s program compulsory for CISCM assessment.
