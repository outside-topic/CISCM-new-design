Public Claims & Brand Use

Say exactly what
has been approved.

Keep recognition statements, titles and CISCM marks accurate, current and limited to the authority formally granted.

Use recognition accurately.

01

Exact award wording

Use the credential title, membership grade or accreditation status actually awarded.

02

Authorized scope

Do not extend claims beyond the approved competence, level or delivery scope.

03

Written mark permission

Use logos and recognition marks only with the required written permission.

04

Current status

Update or remove claims when recognition expires, is suspended or is withdrawn.

05

No implied endorsement

Avoid implying approvals or endorsements that were not granted.

06

Verifiable claims

Ensure public recognition can be checked through CISCM.
