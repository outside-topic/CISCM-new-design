Corporate Academies

Connect business priorities
with measurable application.

Build job-relevant supply chain pathways around organizational strategy, role requirements and a transparent competence standard.

CCISCM

Design the academy around performance.

Define the business need

Specify the performance problem and intended business outcome.

Set role requirements

Identify target roles and required proficiency.

Diagnose capability

Map gaps across teams or learner populations.

Design the pathway

Sequence learning, practice and workplace application.

Build the assessment

Use valid assessment and workplace evidence.

Prepare the people

Develop trainers, coaches, assessors and managers.

Measure transfer

Examine application, performance and sustained learning transfer.

Review and improve

Use evidence to strengthen academy design and governance.

Potential academy outputs.

01

Role and capability architecture

Role profiles, proficiency expectations and learning pathways.

02

Curriculum and assessment

Academy curriculum maps, assessment blueprints and rubrics.

03

People and guidance

Trainer and assessor standards, learner and manager guides.

04

Governance and evidence

Quality assurance arrangements and dashboards covering competence, application and business indicators.
