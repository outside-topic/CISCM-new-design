Complaints

Raise a concern.
Make the evidence clear.

Use the complaints route for concerns about service, quality, conduct or public claims. A challenge to a formal decision belongs in the appeals route.

Prepare a complaint enquiry →View appeals

What to include.

01

Your identity

Your name and contact details.

02

The concern

A clear description of what happened and the concern raised.

03

Supporting evidence

Relevant documents, records and dates where available.

04

The subject

Identify the person, credential or organization involved.

How complaints are handled.

Acknowledgement

Receive and acknowledge the complaint within the applicable period.

Initial assessment

Determine whether the matter falls within CISCM’s scope.

Investigation

Gather and assess evidence and contact relevant parties.

Findings

Prepare findings based on the evidence.

Decision

Communicate the outcome to the relevant parties.

Remedy

Determine appropriate action where a complaint is upheld.

Closure

Close the matter and retain the required records.

Possible outcomes.

  • Correction of public claims or marketing
  • A warning, further evidence or additional training
  • Suspension or withdrawal of recognition
  • Referral to another body where appropriate
  • No action if the complaint is not upheld
