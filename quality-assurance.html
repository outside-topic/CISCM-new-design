Quality Assurance

Confidence beyond
the certificate.

Quality assurance connects eligibility and evidence review with decisions, verification, monitoring, change control, renewal and closure.

Principles behind the decision.

01

Evidence-based

Use documented evidence against stated requirements.

02

Consistent

Treat comparable cases in a comparable way.

03

Transparent

Make the criteria and processes clear.

04

Independent

Separate assessment from commercial and advocacy interests.

05

Proportionate

Match controls to recognition scope and risk.

06

Documented

Maintain decisions and records for review.

Monitoring in practice.

  • Annual returns and desk review of public claims and evidence
  • Observation of delivery, assessment or moderation
  • Site and systems validation
  • Learner, employer, trainer, partner or member sampling
  • Thematic review of online assessment, AI, subcontracting or certification risks
  • Triggered review following concerns, incidents, anomalies or unreported changes

Renewal is a fresh evidence decision.

Notification

Confirm requirements and deadlines.

Evidence submission

Provide current evidence of competence and practice.

Review

Review evidence against applicable standards.

Decision

Document the renewal outcome and rationale.

Renewal is not automatic. Holders must demonstrate continuing competence and compliance.
