The accreditation lifecycle.

Enquiry

Confirm initial eligibility and the intended route.

Application

Declare the exact scope and confirm fees.

Completeness and risk

Check documentation and classify review risk.

Evidence review

Examine records and seek clarification.

Validation

Use interviews, sampling, observation or visits where required.

Draft findings

Allow a factual-accuracy response.

Corrective action

Address identified nonconformity.

Independent decision

Confirm the outcome and permitted scope.

Controlled award

Issue the agreement, decision, certificate and register entry as appropriate.

Ongoing monitoring

Manage annual returns, changes, renewal or closure.

Prepare for a focused review.

  • Consistent legal identity, ownership, authority and operating names
  • Precisely defined programs, sites, modes, languages, territories and partners
  • Controlled policies with current evidence of implementation
  • An accountable coordinator and leadership commitment
  • Public claims consistent with current authority
CISCM accreditation is a professional-learning recognition decision. It should not imply governmental approval, academic degree authority, statutory licensure, immigration eligibility or guaranteed credit transfer without independent confirmation.
