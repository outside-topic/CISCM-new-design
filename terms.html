CISCM services

CISCM describes professional credentials, membership, accreditation, learning and related services. Certification, membership and accreditation have distinct purposes and governing requirements.

User responsibilities

  • Provide accurate and authentic information
  • Follow assessment and membership requirements
  • Use credentials and recognition accurately
  • Respect intellectual property
  • Avoid misleading public claims

Recognition and outcomes

Payment does not guarantee certification, membership grade, accreditation, a passing result or commercial outcomes. Recognition does not guarantee employment, promotion, salary, immigration approval, academic admission or regulatory acceptance.

CISCM may request further evidence or take appropriate action where requirements are not met.
